# Cue: Coach Confidently

Cue is an iPhone app for fitness coaches. Build a class as a sequence of timed exercises, grouped into sections, then run it with a timer that shows what's now and what's next, and buzzes at each transition so you can keep your eyes on the room.

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your iPhone. Cue has no accounts and no servers. See the [privacy policy](privacy/).

**Is my data backed up?**
Cue doesn't back anything up itself. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, Cue's data is included in that backup along with your other apps.

**What happens if I delete the app?**
Your sequences, exercise library and settings are deleted with it. To keep a sequence, share it to yourself first (see below).

**How do I share a sequence?**
Long-press a sequence on the home screen and choose Share, or tap the share button on a sequence. Cue makes a link with the whole sequence in it. Anyone with Cue can open the link, or copy it and tap Import on the home screen, to get their own copy.

**Why don't I feel the haptic buzzes?**
Check that Haptic Feedback is on in Cue's Settings, and that System Haptics is on in the iPhone's Settings > Sounds & Haptics. The Simulator and some older iPhones don't vibrate.

**Does the screen stay on during a workout?**
Yes, while the timer is open.

**Can I see the tour again?**
Yes: Settings > View App Tour.
