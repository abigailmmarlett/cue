# Privacy Policy

**Effective October 18, 2026**

Cue: Coach Confidently ("Cue") is made by Abigail Marlett. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Cue. The short version: **Cue doesn't collect any information about you, and nothing you put in it leaves your iPhone unless you choose to share it.**

## What Cue stores

Everything you create in Cue (your sequences, sections, exercises, exercise library, tags, load icons and settings) is stored in a database on your iPhone. It isn't sent to me or to anyone else.

Cue has no accounts and no servers. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data.

## Sharing a sequence

When you choose to share a sequence, Cue creates a link that contains that sequence: its name, sections, exercises, durations, notes, tags, loads and variations. You decide where to send the link, using the iPhone's share sheet. Cue doesn't upload it anywhere, but anyone you send it to, or who sees it, can read the sequence in it.

When you open a shared link, or tap Import to read one you've copied, Cue reads the link and saves a copy of the sequence on your iPhone. Cue only reads your clipboard when you tap Import.

## Backups

If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or to a computer, Cue's data is included in that backup like your other apps' data. Those backups are handled by Apple and your own settings, not by Cue.

## Deleting your data

You can delete sequences, exercises and tags in the app. Deleting Cue from your iPhone deletes all of its data.

## Children

Cue doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, for example because a future version adds a feature that sends data off your iPhone, I'll update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
